Crownmate is a chess app that runs in your browser (a web app). This policy explains what data the app processes, why, on what legal basis, and what rights you have.

2. At a glance

  • Without an account, your progress is stored only in your browser, not on a server. Technically necessary requests (loading the app, section 3, and the request for the subscription offer, section 9) still happen.
  • An account is optional. It lets you back up your progress in the cloud (servers in Frankfurt).
  • Sharing games and usage data is optional, off by default, and can be turned off at any time.
  • No advertising, no advertising trackers, and no sale of data. Data is passed on only where a service has to do something for you (sections 3 to 8).
  • You can download your data and delete your account yourself in the app at any time.

3. Web app hosting (GitHub Pages)

The app is served through GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the app, your browser sends technically necessary data to GitHub's servers, including your IP address, the date and time, the requested file, and your browser's user agent. GitHub may store this data in server logs. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the app securely and reliably (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO, the EU General Data Protection Regulation, or GDPR).

GitHub also processes data in the USA. According to GitHub, it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (the list of certified companies is at dataprivacyframework.gov); for transfers to countries without an adequacy decision, GitHub uses the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914). See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

4. Storage on your device (without cookies)

Crownmate stores settings, progress, games, and analyses in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and caches the app files for offline use (Service Worker). This data stays on your device unless you create an account or turn on sharing. The app does not set cookies. Fonts, images, and sounds are supplied with the app, not by external servers (for example, not by Google Fonts). Local storage is strictly necessary for the app to function (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG, Germany's law on data protection in telecommunications and digital services). “Reset progress” in the settings deletes your progress, games and analyses; your settings, your sign-in and the other data below remain. If you are signed in and this device is synced with the cloud, the empty progress then also replaces your cloud backup; if it is not synced yet, the app then loads your cloud progress. You delete all data of this app in this browser through your browser's website data settings; your account and your cloud backup remain (deleting the account: section 5).

With an account, the device also stores your sign-in (session token with your account details: email address, with Google also your name, profile picture address and Google ID, with Apple your Apple ID identifier and, if shared, your name), a fallback copy of your progress before an import from the cloud, a marker showing whether this device is already synced with the cloud, subscription and age markers, the record of your consent and a queue of data not yet transmitted. If you follow an invitation link, the device remembers the pending online invitation, even without an account. These data are used only for the respective function. “Sign out” removes the sign-in from this browser (other devices and browsers stay signed in); the other entries remain until you delete the website data in your browser.

The app also keeps a list of its latest technical errors (at most 50; it removes addresses, email addresses and identifiers first). This list stays on your device and is never sent automatically. You can see it in the settings under “Report for the test phase”, copy or share it yourself there, and delete it with “Clear error list”. Separately, if you have turned on sharing of usage data with an account, the app transmits errors as usage events (section 6).

5. Account (optional)

With an account, your progress is backed up in the cloud so you can continue on more than one device. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code, or with your Google account; once the app offers it, also with your Apple ID.

What data

  • Your email address and sign-in times,
  • when signing in with Google: your email address, name, profile image URL as provided by your Google profile, and a Google user identifier,
  • when signing in with Apple: your email address (or an Apple relay address if you choose to hide it), your name if you share it, and an Apple user identifier,
  • a username you choose,
  • if you add friends: who is friends with whom or has sent a request (with the time), your invite code (valid for 7 days) and your weekly score (points and number of scored games per week). Your friends only see your username, your weekly score and your profile, never your email address. If you decline a request, it is kept for 30 days so the same person cannot ask you again during that time; after that it is deleted,
  • your progress (settings, progress, games, and analyses as stored by the app),
  • the browser family and operating system of the device used for the most recent backup (for example, “iPadOS Safari”), rather than the full user agent,
  • your device's IP address: Supabase stores it in technical server logs with every request, and in the authentication log together with the time and sign-in method whenever you sign in, to protect your account.

Bot protection at sign-in (Cloudflare Turnstile, once switched on): To stop programs from creating accounts or triggering sign-in emails in bulk, a service from Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, checks whether a human is using the sign-in. The app loads the check only when you open the sign-in in the settings, never at start. According to Cloudflare (Turnstile privacy addendum, last updated 18 June 2025), it also uses the same characteristics under its own responsibility to improve its bot detection; Cloudflare itself is the controller for that. You can do so at any time in the app under Settings → Data → Delete account. We then immediately delete everything associated with your account in our database: the account and authentication data (including the Google or Apple link), username, friendships, requests, invite code and weekly score, cloud progress, shared games and usage data, the authentication log and the administration log, plus everything named in the sections below (player card, league, puzzle duels, online games, school mode), your subscription status (section 7) and the coach counters (section 8). Details are in the sections "Shop: editions" and "Pioneer title" below. Supabase's technical server logs and backups cannot be accessed immediately: depending on the plan, Supabase keeps server logs for at most 7 days and backups, where the plan includes them, also for at most 7 days. They are then deleted automatically and are not used further in the meantime. Your progress on your device is unaffected.

Player card, achievements and thalers

With an account, we store which achievement tiers (bronze, silver, gold) you have reached, the thalers those tiers credited to you, which banners you own and what your player card looks like (banner, a title from a fixed list or a special title such as "Creator" or "Pioneer" that you own, profile picture, background, frame and up to three achievements on show). Without an account, all of this stays on your device. This data is necessary for the app's features (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR) and is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, just like your account.

Your player card is visible only to the person playing the same online game with you (invited or via matchmaking): your username, title, banner, profile picture, league tier with its symbol, the achievements on show, your best season badge and a season title if you have chosen one (see "Seasons") and, in rated games, your online Elo. Games with friends remain without Elo. Special titles such as "Creator" or "Pioneer" (with a number) are seen only by your opponent in a shared online game, and only if the server confirms that you own them; otherwise the app shows them only on your own card. Your weekly league group and your opponents in the puzzle duel cannot see it, nor can other users or visitors without an account. Download my data includes this data. If your account is deleted, it is deleted automatically too.

League with crowns and weekly groups

With an account, you take part in the league. For this we store:

  • your league tier (Squire III to Crownmate I), your total crowns and an estimate of your playing strength (Elo) with the number of games it is based on,
  • every crown entry with its source (online game, bot ladder, AI opponent, starting league, puzzle duel against a person or against the AI), the ID of the game or duel, the change, the week and the time. The limits against abuse (how many games count per day and how many crowns from bot and AI games count per week at most) are derived from these entries; there are no separate counters,
  • for each week your group (week, tier), your crowns in that week and when you joined. You only join a group with your first crown of the week,
  • the reward for your place at the end of the week (week, tier, place, type and amount) and whether it has been credited,
  • during matchmaking: that you are searching, since when, with which time control and your playing strength (Elo), so that an opponent of similar strength can be found. If no person is found, you receive a voucher for a game against the AI opponent.

Who sees what: The up to 30 members of your weekly group see your username (without a username it says "Player"), your profile picture (piece, background and frame), the banner you wear, your place, your crowns this week and your tier. They see nothing else, in particular not your email address, your title, the achievements you show, your Elo or your individual entries. Free places in the group are filled by computer-controlled players; they are marked "AI", and no person is behind them. Other groups and visitors without an account cannot see your group.

This data is necessary for the league (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR) and is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, just like your account. The database itself calculates the weekly result (place, promotion and relegation, reward). We delete a matchmaking search after 2 minutes without a partner and unused AI vouchers after 6 hours, each at the next clean-up. Past weekly groups, your places in them and your rewards are deleted automatically once the week lies more than 52 weeks (one year) back, each at the next clean-up. Banners from those weeks are added to your collection first; thalers from those weeks that have not been paid out yet expire. Your tier, your crown total and your entries remain stored until you delete your account; they are then deleted automatically. Download my data includes everything that is stored at that time.

Seasons

The online league runs in seasons of 8 weeks each; the first one starts on 4 January 2027. At the end of a season the database itself settles it and stores for every account that played in it:

  • a badge with the number of the season and the best league tier you reached in it,
  • a season title if you earned it: "Crownmaster" for the tier Crownmate I, "Top 10" or "Top 100" for the places by online Elo among all accounts with at least 20 online games with crowns in that season (rated games with at least 4 half-moves, at most 3 per opponent and day),
  • which of your season titles you want to show; without a choice none is shown.

When the new season begins, your league tier drops by 3 tiers (at most down to Squire III). We do not publish a ranking with names: the places by Elo are only used to award the titles. Your best badge and your chosen title are seen only by the person playing the same online game with you (see "Player card"); only you see your own badges and titles in your profile. This data is necessary for the league (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR), is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt just like your account and remains stored until you delete your account; it is then deleted automatically. Download my data includes it.

Puzzle duel

In the puzzle duel, two players solve the same chess puzzles for three minutes. With an account, we store for each duel the type (rated, between friends or against the AI), the account IDs of both players, for a duel between friends the invitation code, the order of the puzzles, start, duration, status and result, and for each player how many puzzles were solved and how many mistakes were made, the number of moves and the time of the last move. During matchmaking we store that you are searching, since when and your playing strength (Elo), so that an opponent of similar strength can be found. Crowns you win are entered as in the league; the daily limits (at most 20 duels against the AI and 3 rated duels against the same person per day) are derived from these entries. Wrongly entered invitation codes are counted as for online games.

Who sees what: During and after the duel, your opponent only sees how many puzzles you solved, how many mistakes you made and when your time ends. They do not see your username, your player card or your email address in the duel. The AI opponent is not a person but is calculated by the server; the app marks it as AI. Uninvolved people cannot see any duel.

This data is necessary for the duel (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR) and is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, just like your account. Your moves are checked by an Edge Function, also in Frankfurt (see "Where and by whom"). Every duel is evaluated on the server: at the next request and additionally every 10 minutes by a scheduler in the database (pg_cron), so that a duel also ends when nobody opens the app any more. We delete a matchmaking search after 2 minutes without a partner and invitations nobody accepted after 24 hours. Finished duels remain stored until one of the two accounts is deleted; the duel is then deleted for both. Download my data includes your duels with your own status in them.

Online games with friends

When you play a friend online, we store both account IDs, the moves, current position, chess clock, invitation code, and game timestamps. Only the two players can read the game; an open invitation is initially visible only to its creator. This data is necessary to play together (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR) and is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, just like your account.

The same applies to games from league matchmaking: there the server pairs you with a person of similar playing strength (instead of an invitation code), and the game counts for the league (section "League with crowns and weekly groups"). What is stored during the search is described there.

Download my data also includes your online games. If either participating account is deleted, its associated online games are automatically deleted too (cascading deletion). Otherwise, games remain stored in the account. Technical server logs and backups follow the retention periods described in section 5.

Protection against misuse: So that nobody can try out invitation codes or flood the database, the server counts, per account and day, how often a wrong invitation code was entered (after 20, joining is blocked until the next day), and, per hour, how many games and usage events your account has shared (section 6). These counters contain only numbers, no content. We delete the failed attempts after two days and the counters of shared data after 48 hours, each at the next automatic cleanup. Until then, the counters of shared data remain even after you withdraw consent; otherwise the limit could be bypassed by deleting and sending again. Invitations that nobody accepted are deleted after 48 hours at the next clean-up. If no move is made for too long in a game (with a clock, 10 minutes before the second half-move; without a clock, 7 days), the server ends it as abandoned, without a result. Both counters are included in Download my data. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO, GDPR; see also recital 49 on network and information security).

School mode (classes)

With an account, a teacher or a club coach can create a class (leader), and others join with a 6-character code (members). For each class we store the name, the code, the leader, whether it is active, and when it was created and closed, plus the class tasks (title, type, goal, due date). For each member we store a self-chosen display name (first name or nickname, not an email address), the day they joined, and one row of figures: bots beaten, stars, number of games, average accuracy, the most frequent error types, completed tasks, and the day (without time) of the last update. We do not store individual games, moves, times of day or device data for this.

Who sees what: The leader sees the display name and figures of the members of their classes, never their email address or account ID. A member sees only their own row and the class tasks, not the other members.

This data is necessary for school mode (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR) and is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, like your account. If you leave a class, your row is deleted; if the leader deletes a class, the whole class is deleted. Closed classes are deleted 90 days after closing at the next clean-up. To prevent codes from being guessed, we count wrong codes per account and day and delete these counters after two days (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO, GDPR). Everything is deleted automatically with your account. If the leader deletes their account, their classes are deleted too, with all member rows, figures and tasks; the members' accounts remain.

Shop: editions

The shop offers limited, numbered editions. They are visible only to people who are signed in and have stated that they are 18 or older. We store this statement as yes or no with a timestamp, without a date of birth.

If you buy an edition, we store: a reservation that holds your item while you are at the Stripe checkout (edition, status, ID and address of the checkout session, payment ID, times); your two declarations on the right of withdrawal (consent to immediate delivery and acknowledgement that the right of withdrawal expires, with text version and time) as evidence; and your ownership (edition, number, status, payment and checkout session IDs, times). Only you can see your ownership. To prevent misuse, we limit reservations to three per account and edition within 24 hours. Stripe payment events for editions (with amount, reason and the state of any refund or chargeback) are deleted after 90 days, except while a refund or a case is still open.

The legal basis is the purchase contract (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR), and in addition Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. c and f DSGVO for evidence of the withdrawal declarations and for retention obligations. Cancelled and expired reservations are deleted 90 days after their last change. Your age statement is deleted with your account. Paid reservations, the withdrawal declarations and ownership remain stored without your account ID after your account is deleted, for as long as the edition exists: this ensures no number is issued twice and that the purchase and declarations remain provable.

Pioneer title

The first 100 accounts receive the title "Pioneer" with a number. For this we store which number was assigned to your account and when (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR). If you delete your account, the number remains without your account ID so that it is never issued a second time.

Administration by the operator

The legal basis for the changes is your user agreement (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR). 6. Sharing games and usage data (optional, with consent)

With an account, you can turn on Share games and usage under Settings → Data. It is off by default. Only after you turn it on does the app transmit the following data:

  • Completed games: moves, move assessments (move classes), result and reason, opponent bot, game mode, accuracy, number of hints and takebacks, duration, timestamp, and app version.
  • Usage events: which area of the app was opened, the start and end of a game, enabling sharing, and app errors (only error type and message, without addresses), each with a timestamp, app version, browser family, and operating system (for example, “iOS Safari”), rather than the full user agent.

Purpose: to calibrate bot strength using real games, improve the coach's move explanations, and offer personal training plans; also to identify where players stop using the app. For bots, the coach, and the app, we evaluate data only in aggregate across all players. Your individual data is used only for your own training plans, which only you can see; it is not used to assess you against others. There are no automated decisions with legal effects (Art. 22 DSGVO, GDPR).

The data is linked to your account (pseudonymous: events contain neither your name nor your email address) so that you can download it and delete it along with your account. It is therefore not anonymous.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO, GDPR). We store when you consented as evidence (Art. 7 Abs. 1 DSGVO). You can withdraw consent at any time for the future by turning the switch off. The app then stops sending data, discards unsent data, and deletes all previously shared games and usage data associated with your account from the server (Art. 17 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO). If you are offline, the app completes the deletion as soon as you are online again and signed in on this device with the same account, before it sends anything else. If you sign out first, turn the switch off once more after your next sign-in, or send us an email. We store the withdrawal time as evidence. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it (Art. 7 Abs. 3 DSGVO).

Retention: The data is tied to your account. It is deleted as soon as you withdraw consent (switch off) or delete your account, whichever comes first.

7. Trainer subscription and Stripe

The optional trainer subscription and purchases in the shop (section "Shop: editions") use Stripe as payment provider. Our contracting partner in the European Economic Area is Stripe Payments Europe, Limited (Ireland). In particular, the account ID, email address, selected product, and payment and transaction data are sent to Stripe. You enter payment details such as card numbers directly with Stripe; Crownmate does not receive them in full. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Stripe processes this data for us as a processor and, as described in its privacy policy, also uses it for its own purposes under its own responsibility, for example to prevent fraud and to meet its own legal obligations. That policy also describes to whom and to which countries Stripe transfers data.

In Supabase we store the state of your subscription (status, plan, valid until, Stripe customer number and subscription ID); it is deleted immediately with your account. So that each Stripe event is processed exactly once, we also store technical payment events (event ID and type, Stripe customer number, subscription ID, status, plan, times). They are deleted at the next incoming event after 90 days; after your account is deleted they remain until then without your account ID. Invoices and payment records are held in our Stripe account. We keep them for as long as statutory retention obligations require (in particular section 147 of the German Fiscal Code (Abgabenordnung), up to 10 years; legal basis Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) and delete them afterwards.

8. Coach using Anthropic

When you have the AI coach explain a move, the server sends Anthropic (our contracting partner in the European Economic Area is Anthropic Ireland, Limited, Ireland) only facts about the position: the position (FEN), the move played and the strongest move, the engine evaluations, the classification of the move, whether you made the move yourself, and your rough playing strength as a word (for example “beginner”). There is no free text from you. Email address, username, and account ID are not sent. Crownmate stores neither the position nor the answer. It only stores how many questions you asked on a given day (to enforce the limit); these counters are deleted after a little more than a week at the next coach request and immediately with the account. According to Anthropic, it deletes requests and responses within 30 days; only if a request is flagged as violating Anthropic's usage policy, after up to 2 years. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Anthropic processes the data as a processor (Art. 28 GDPR); the data processing agreement is part of Anthropic's commercial terms. Anthropic may also process the data in the USA; the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2), which are part of that agreement, apply.

9. Public product request

When you open the “Crownmate Trainer” page, the app asks Supabase which subscription is offered, always without your account ID, even when you are signed in. This technically places the IP address, time, and connection data in server logs. The product request contains no account ID. Supabase deletes the server logs after at most 7 days (section 5).

10. Scope of data sharing and retention

The “Share games and usage” switch controls only the voluntarily shared game archive and usage events. It does not control the account, cloud progress, online games, subscription, coach, or technically necessary logs.

Online games follow the periods in section 5: unanswered invitations are deleted after 48 hours at the next clean-up, completed games remain until one of the two accounts is deleted. Supabase deletes server logs and backups after at most 7 days (section 5). Everything else remains stored until the period named in the individual sections ends or you delete your account.

11. What never happens

  • No advertising or advertising trackers (no Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or fingerprinting).
  • No tracking across websites.
  • No disclosure to third parties for their own purposes and no sale of data. The processors listed above act only on our instructions. For the subscription and the shop, Stripe receives the data needed for the payment and partly also uses it under its own responsibility (section 7).
  • No profiles about you for other companies.

12. Children and parents

Crownmate can be used at any age without an account; your progress then stays on the device. You can create an account from the age of 13. If you are under 16, ask your parents or legal guardians before creating an account. You can give consent yourself, for example to sharing games and usage data (section 6), only from the age of 16; before that, only with your parents' approval (Art. 8(1) DSGVO, GDPR). The switch is therefore off by default, and the app explains this right beside it. Editions in the shop are available only from 18 (section "Shop: editions"). Parents can request information about an account or its deletion at kontakt@crownmate.de.

13. Your rights

  • Access (Art. 15) and data portability (Art. 20): in the app under Settings → Data → Download my data, you receive a file with all data in our database that belongs to your account. We will tell you by email, on request, what Supabase, Stripe or Anthropic hold beyond that.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): use Delete account in the app, immediately for everything in our database except the exceptions in section 5; Supabase server logs and backups expire according to their retention periods (see section 5).
  • Rectification (Art. 16): change your username in the app; request any other corrections by email.
  • Restriction (Art. 18) and objection (Art. 21): email the address given above.
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7 Abs. 3): turn off Share games and usage; previously shared data is then deleted from the server.
  • Complaint (Art. 77): contact a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW), Kavalleriestrasse 2-4, 40213 Duesseldorf.

The article references in this section refer to the DSGVO (GDPR).

14. Security

All connections are encrypted (TLS). On the server, row-level access rules (Row Level Security) ensure that each account can create and read only its own data; an account can only create shared usage data, which can be read only through the data access request. The app contains only a public key; secrets are held exclusively by the operator.

15. Changes

This policy is updated when the app or applicable law changes. The current version is linked in the app under Settings → Info.
