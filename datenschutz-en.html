Crownmate is a chess app that runs in your browser (a web app). This policy explains what data the app processes, why, on what legal basis, and what rights you have.

2. At a glance

  • Without an account, your progress is stored only in your browser, not on a server. Technically necessary requests (loading the app, section 3, and the request for the subscription offer, section 9) still happen.
  • An account is optional. It lets you back up your progress in the cloud (servers in Frankfurt).
  • Sharing games and usage data is optional, off by default, and can be turned off at any time.
  • No advertising, no advertising trackers, and no sale of data. Data is passed on only where a service has to do something for you (sections 3 to 8).
  • You can download your data and delete your account yourself in the app at any time.

4. Storage on your device (without cookies)

Crownmate stores settings, progress, games, and analyses in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and caches the app files for offline use (Service Worker). This data stays on your device unless you create an account or turn on sharing. The app does not set cookies. Fonts, images, and sounds are supplied with the app, not by external servers (for example, not by Google Fonts). Local storage is strictly necessary for the app to function (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG, Germany's law on data protection in telecommunications and digital services). “Reset progress” in the settings deletes your progress, games and analyses; your settings, your sign-in and the other data below remain. If you are signed in and this device has been matched with the cloud, the empty progress then also replaces your cloud backup. “Delete all app data on this device” removes your progress, your settings and all account data on this device; if you are signed in, the app backs up to the cloud once more first. Only the app's program files (for starting without a connection, no personal data), a marker showing which areas are unlocked, and a pending withdrawal of your data sharing, which the app completes later (section 6), remain. Your account and your cloud backup remain (deleting the account: section 5). You can also delete the data through your browser's website data settings.

With an account, the device also stores your sign-in (session token with your account details: email address, with Google also your name, profile picture address and Google ID), a fallback copy of your progress before an import from the cloud, a marker showing whether this device has already been matched with the cloud, subscription and age markers, the record of your consent and a queue of data not yet transmitted. If you follow an invitation link, the device remembers the pending online invitation, even without an account. These data are used only for the respective function. “Sign out and remove account data from this device” deletes them (other devices stay signed in); your progress on the device remains, as do a pending withdrawal and the marker for unlocked areas.

The app also keeps a list of its latest technical errors (at most 50; it removes addresses, email addresses and identifiers first). This list stays on your device and is never sent automatically. You can see it in the settings under “Report for the test phase”, copy or share it yourself there, and delete it with “Clear error list”. Separately, if you have turned on sharing of usage data with an account, the app transmits errors as usage events (section 6).

With an account, your progress is backed up in the cloud so you can continue on more than one device. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code, or with your Google account.

Legal basis and retention

Processing is necessary to provide the account (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR). The data is retained until you delete your account. You can do so at any time in the app under Settings → Data → Delete account. We then immediately delete everything associated with your account in our database: the account and authentication data (including the Google link), username, cloud progress, shared games and usage data, and the authentication log. Exception for the subscription: technical payment events from Stripe (section 7) remain without your account ID but with the Stripe customer number until they are deleted at the next incoming payment event after 90 days. A running subscription does not end with the account; cancel it first (the app points this out when you delete the account). Supabase's technical server logs and backups cannot be accessed immediately: they are deleted automatically according to Supabase's retention periods [Check Supabase retention periods: server logs, backups] and are not used further in the meantime. Your progress on your device is unaffected.

Online games with friends

When you play a friend online, we store both account IDs, the moves, current position, chess clock, invitation code, and game timestamps. Only the two players can read the game; an open invitation is initially visible only to its creator. This data is necessary to play together (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, GDPR) and is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, just like your account.

Download my data also includes your online games. If either participating account is deleted, its associated online games are automatically deleted too (cascading deletion). Otherwise, games remain stored in the account. Technical server logs and backups follow the retention periods described in section 5.

Protection against misuse: So that nobody can try out invitation codes or flood the database, the server counts, per account and day, how often a wrong invitation code was entered (after 20, joining is blocked until the next day), and, per hour, how many games and usage events your account has shared (section 6). These counters contain only numbers, no content. We delete the failed attempts after two days and the counters of shared data after 48 hours, each at the next automatic cleanup. Until then, the counters of shared data remain even after you withdraw consent; otherwise the limit could be bypassed by deleting and sending again. Invitations that nobody accepted are deleted after 48 hours. If no move is made for too long in a game (with a clock, 10 minutes before the second half-move; without a clock, 7 days), the server ends it as abandoned, without a result. Both counters are included in Download my data. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO, GDPR) [Have legal basis and retention periods confirmed by the expert].

6. Sharing games and usage data (optional, with consent)

With an account, you can turn on Share games and usage under Settings → Data. It is off by default. Only after you turn it on does the app transmit the following data:

  • Completed games: moves, move assessments (move classes), result and reason, opponent bot, game mode, accuracy, number of hints and takebacks, duration, timestamp, and app version.
  • Usage events: which area of the app was opened, the start and end of a game, enabling sharing, and app errors (only error type and message, without addresses), each with a timestamp, app version, browser family, and operating system (for example, “iOS Safari”), rather than the full user agent.

Purpose: to calibrate bot strength using real games, improve the coach's move explanations, and offer personal training plans; also to identify where players stop using the app. For bots, the coach, and the app, we evaluate data only in aggregate across all players. Your individual data is used only for your own training plans, which only you can see; it is not used to assess you against others. There are no automated decisions with legal effects (Art. 22 DSGVO, GDPR).

The data is linked to your account (pseudonymous: events contain neither your name nor your email address) so that you can download it and delete it along with your account. It is therefore not anonymous.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO, GDPR). We store when you consented as evidence (Art. 7 Abs. 1 DSGVO). You can withdraw consent at any time for the future by turning the switch off. The app then stops sending data, discards unsent data, and deletes all previously shared games and usage data associated with your account from the server (Art. 17 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO). If you are offline, the app completes this as soon as a connection is available, before it sends anything else. We store the withdrawal time as evidence. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it (Art. 7 Abs. 3 DSGVO).

Retention: The data is tied to your account and deleted with it. Independently of that, shared games and usage data are deleted or aggregated so they can no longer be linked to an account after at most [For example, 24 months].

14. Security

All connections are encrypted (TLS). On the server, row-level access rules (Row Level Security) ensure that each account can create and read only its own data; an account can only create shared usage data, which can be read only through the data access request. The app contains only a public key; secrets are held exclusively by the operator.
