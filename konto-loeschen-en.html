Delete account

Here you can delete your Crownmate account, even without the app. In the app it is under Settings → Data → “Delete account”.

Straight to “Delete it yourself”

What is deleted

Immediately and for good on the server:

  • your account with its email address and sign-in data (including the link to Google or Apple)
  • your username, friendships, requests, invite code and weekly ranking
  • your cloud progress
  • shared games and usage data
  • the sign-in log
  • your player card with achievements and thalers, your league with crowns and weekly groups, your puzzle duels (for both players), your online games, your data in school mode and the age statement for the shop

What stays: the progress on your devices. Supabase server logs and backups are deleted automatically after at most 7 days. Technical payment events from Stripe remain without your account ID but with the Stripe customer number until they are deleted at the next incoming payment event after 90 days. For shop editions and the pioneer title, a few details stay without your account ID, as described in the privacy policy.

Subscription: A running subscription does not end with the account. Cancel it first.

Delete it yourself

Sign in first

So that nobody deletes someone else’s account, you sign in first, just like in the app.

Signed in as

Really delete for good? This cannot be undone. You can sign up again later with the same email address, but with empty cloud progress.

Your account has been deleted.

You are signed out. The progress on your devices stays until you delete it there.

Delete by email

Can’t sign in any more? Then write from your account’s address to kontakt@crownmate.de with the subject “Crownmate: Delete account”. Give the account’s email address and, if you know it, your username. We delete the account within one month and confirm it to you by email.
