# English glossary

- **Stage** is a bot's numbered place on the opponent ladder.
- **Level** is the player's long-term progress earned with sparks.
- **Sparks** are the experience points earned through play and learning.
- **World badge** is the award for collecting every star in one world.
- **Daily goal** is the calm, optional playing goal for the current local day.
- **Takeback** is the action that returns a game to the position before the player's last move.
- **Playing strength** is the estimated chess strength shown after enough meaningful moves.
- **Final boss** is the last bot in a world and can be challenged at any time once that world is open.
